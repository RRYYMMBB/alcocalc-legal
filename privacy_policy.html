Privacy Policy — Alcohol Calculator

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · Published by Mykhailo Buryak

What stays on your device

Your profile (sex, weight), country, units, and drink/session history are stored locally on your device. The app works offline and does not send this data to the developer's servers or share it with third parties.

Notifications

Local notifications about your estimated alcohol level are scheduled and shown by your device itself, with no server involved.

Analytics and ads

The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect device identifiers or track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Because the core data lives only on your device, you control it directly through the app's settings.

Children

This app is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
